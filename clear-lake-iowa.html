Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Cleaning in Clear Lake

How do I know if I need main sewer line cleaning?

If several drains clog or run slow at the same time, toilets gurgle when other fixtures are used, or wastewater backs up into your basement floor drain, the blockage is typically located in your main sewer line.

What are the benefits of hydro jetting over standard snaking?

While standard snaking punches a hole through a clog to restore basic flow, hydro jetting uses high-pressure water streams to thoroughly clean grease, scale, and buildup from the entire inner circumference of the pipe, significantly reducing the chances of recurring clogs.
