Signs Your Main Sewer Line May Be Clogged

When the main sewer line leaving your home becomes blocked, wastewater cannot leave the house normally. This can cause backups in the lowest drains and plumbing fixtures.

Common signs include:

  • Water coming up from a basement floor drain when toilets, sinks, or laundry are used.
  • Multiple slow drains throughout the home instead of only one clogged fixture.
  • Toilets backing up or gurgling when other plumbing is running.
  • Sewer odors coming from drains.

What Causes Main Sewer Lines To Plug Up in Mason City?

Main sewer line problems can happen for different reasons. Older homes and sewer systems can develop issues over time from roots, buildup, shifting, or damaged pipes.

  • Tree roots in sewer lines: Roots can enter small openings in older sewer pipes and restrict water flow.
  • Grease and buildup: Over time, material can collect inside the line and reduce the space available for wastewater to move.
  • Pipe settling or damage: Shifting soil or older pipes can create areas where debris collects.
  • Flushed items that should not go down the toilet: Wipes, paper towels, and other materials can contribute to blockages.

What To Do Before Main Sewer Line Cleaning

If you have a sewer backup happening in your home, these steps can help reduce additional water entering the line:

  • Stop using washing machines, dishwashers, and other large water sources.
  • Avoid repeatedly flushing toilets that are backing up.
  • Do not pour chemical drain cleaners into a backed-up sewer line.
  • Move belongings away from basement drains if it is safe to do so.
